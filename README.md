# Age Calculator

This project is a simple Age Calculator that allows users to enter their birth date and calculates their current age based on the current date.

## Features

- **Input Field:** Enter your birth date in the input field.
- **Calculate Button:** After entering the date, click the "Calculate" button to see your age.
- **Display Age:** The app displays the calculated age in years, months, and days.

## Usage

1. **Enter Your Birth Date:**
   - Use the input field to enter your birth date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

2. **Calculate Your Age:**
   - Click the "Calculate" button to compute your age based on the current date.

3. **View Your Age:**
   - After clicking "Calculate," your age will be displayed in years, months, and days.

## Example Output:

- **Birth Date:** 1990-05-15
- **Age:** You are 34 years, 3 months, and 24 days.
